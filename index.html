Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the SimpLift mobile application.

Data Collection and Storage

We respect your privacy. All user-entered data, including workout routines, exercise logs, and body measurements, is stored locally on your device (using a local SQLite database). We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on external servers.

Third-Party Services

The Application does not share, sell, or disclose any of your data to third parties, advertising networks, or analytics providers.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data is stored exclusively on your device, uninstalling the application or clearing its app storage will permanently remove all your stored data.
